06 About Zach

Hi, I'm Zach Noka.

I'm a Rhode Island photographer who loves making pictures with a point of view. Yes, I'm also the guy hiding behind a rock at your proposal, trying not to cry.

Most people get nervous about the same things: where to stand, where to look, what to do with their hands. That's my favorite part. I'll direct you through all of it, try a new idea or two, play with the light, and keep pushing until we've made something worth framing.

Some days that's clean and timeless. Other days it's bold, moody or a little dramatic. Either way, you leave feeling confident, and trust me, that always shows in the photos.
